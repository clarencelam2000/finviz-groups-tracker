Five ways to open a chart

The live Show chart ▾ pill on Lookup, Picks, Morning and Positions measures roughly 34×24pt — under Apple's 44×44pt minimum, and stuck in the one corner a thumb has to stretch for. Each mock below is built at the card's real width (336pt) with the actual copy from a live Picks row (GH 86 · Guardant Health). Tap any card to feel the toggle; the dashed box is the tap zone, sized to scale.

Pairing

Ship 02 as the default, keep 01 as the fallback cue

Make the whole header row the toggle everywhere charts appear — Lookup, Picks, Morning, Positions — since it needs no new element and the largest realistic target wins on a thumb. Leave a small chevron sitting where the pill is today, padded to the same 44pt minimum as option 01, purely as a visual "there's more here" cue — it rides along for free once the row itself is already listening for the tap.
