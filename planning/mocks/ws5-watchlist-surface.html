Personal watchlist — surface mock

A pre-position radar: add a ticker, it rides the next N morning scrapes and shows a real morning status card, then graduates to a position in one tap. Rendered in the shipping PWA's visual language (slate-900 / sky-400) so it translates 1:1 into docs/index.html. Deliberately dark-only — the app is single-theme dark.

The load-bearing idea

A watch item is not a trade ticket. A stop is a position-management concept — it's born at entry, not before, and it depends on the fill you get on a day you can't predict. So a watch item has no stop and no size, ever. It carries a ticker and, optionally, a level of interest. The full ticket (entry · stop · size) appears at exactly one moment: "I took it." That's the only place the §8a form is reused.

the add path

One field. Not a form.

  • Required = ticker. Type it, tap Watch, done. The prev-eng "same form as §8a" is dropped for the add path — a full trade ticket to watch something is nonsense.
  • Optional level of interest — a single price + above/below. Drives a "your level" read on the card. No stop. No size. Those don't exist until you're in.
  • Manage here, view on Morning. Add/remove/renew are D1 writes → they sit on the authed Positions tab (where the manual ticket already lives). The status cards you actually read each morning render on the Morning tab (§2). Mirrors how picks work: selected in the backend world, viewed on Morning.
  • TTL. Each entry rides N mornings then drops out of the scrape. Expired items fall into a collapsed bin with one-tap Renew; auto-purged after ~14 days so nothing silently vanishes and the active list stays clean.
Owner call — N default. Mock shows N=10 (≈2 weeks). Enough for a breakout setup to trigger or go stale? Renew resets to N.
Owner call — manage location. Positions tab (shown) vs. a small manage strip on Morning itself. Solo user + low-PII, so either works.

the morning read

Both reads, when you've set a level.

  • System read (always). Uses prior High as the trigger and prior Low as the floor — the exact same engine as pick cards (compute_pick_status). Full vocabulary, including the honest "back below yesterday's low" downside — labeled as a range break, not a "stop" (there's no position to stop out of).
  • Your-level read (when set). A trigger-only read vs. your price — "crossed ✓" / "2.2% away". No floor, because a watch item has no stop.
  • Actionable cards (Triggered / Gapped) surface the entry-quality metric (ATR-from-low) and a prominent I took it →, exactly like pick cards do.
  • No MAs needed. The whole read is High / Low / ATR — all in the private ticker_quotes feed already. MAs live in that feed's raw blob too, so richer chips (Focus / launch-ready) are possible later without new plumbing.
Owner call — watch cards: minimal (shown) or full pick adornments? I kept them lean (status + your level). Focus score / launch-ready chips are derivable but add noise to a radar.

This is the one place the §8a form is reused — because now a stop and size are real. The ticket opens prefilled with the ticker (and your level as an entry hint); you set the stop and size against today's actual structure, then it becomes a position.

the unification, done right

Same payload, at the right moment.

  • Prefilled, not pre-decided. Ticker carries over; your watch level seeds the entry field. Stop and size are blank and required — you fill them against today's structure, which is the whole reason they weren't asked at watch time.
  • Reuses the shipped path. Confirm → POST /positions with the exact §8a manual payload (meta.source can note 'watchlist'). Zero new write path; the position list already renders it.
  • Graduation clears the watch entry — it's a position now, and rides the held feed / lifecycle engine from here.
Confirms the prev-eng instinct was half right: the form unifies at graduation, not at add. That's the reframe this mock is built on.
