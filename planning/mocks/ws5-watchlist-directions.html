Watchlist — pick a direction

The first mock was anchored on the current app's cramped card styling. This is a clean-sheet exploration: three card treatments to choose one, four ways to show both reads, the full enum-state gallery, and the two add affordances. Dark-committed (the app is single-theme). Numbers are monospace throughout; one cool accent, semantic color only for status. Mix a direction with any tweaks — none of this is locked.

01

Same two names in each: CRWD (triggered, with a level set) and FTNT (setting up, level set). Judge legibility, density, and how loud the chrome feels.

My lean: B for the Morning view, C as a "compact mode" toggle. B gives each watched name the clarity of a real setup read (the gauge unifies both reads visually); C is what you'll want once the list grows past ~8. A is elegant but its quietness fights the "tell me what needs attention this morning" job. Pick one to start — the others are cheap to add later.
02

System read + your-level read — how they coexist

The system read (always, from the shared morning engine) and the your-level read (only when you set a level). Four ways to surface both without clutter.

iStacked lines
CRWDTriggered
system broke 155.10 · +2.1%
your 156 crossed ✓
Explicit, unambiguous. Costs vertical height. (What the v1 mock did.)
iiHeadline swap
CRWDYour 156 ✓
crossed your level · system: triggered, +2.1%
When you set a level, it becomes the headline; the system read demotes to a sub-line. Puts your thesis first.
iiiMerged sentence
CRWDTriggered
Broke 155.10 and crossed your 156 · +2.1%
One natural line. Compact and human; slightly less scannable at a glance.
ivGauge (visual)
CRWDTriggered
low
high
you
Both reads become one picture: price dot vs. prior-low, prior-high, and your level. Scales to MA-reclaim (add a "50MA" marker). My pick, paired with a one-line caption.
MA-reclaim fits here natively: a structural level ("reclaim the 50MA") is just another marker on the gauge (iv) or another clause in the sentence (iii) — the 50MA recovered from the private feed's raw blob. That's the phase-2 level type you asked for.
