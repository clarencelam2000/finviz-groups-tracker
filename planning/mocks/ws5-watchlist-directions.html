The predicate

  • Reclaim = day_low < ref AND price > ref, where ref = prior low, 20MA, or 50MA.
  • Mirror of failed_breakout (tagged the high, fell back). This is: dipped below support, recovered above it — a bullish reversal read.
  • Lives in pick_status.py, so picks inherit it — the "add once, everywhere" property you asked me to protect.
